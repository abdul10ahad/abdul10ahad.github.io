Privacy Policy

SE Tracker · Effective 2 November 2026

SE Tracker has no accounts and no servers of its own. The developer does not receive your watches, notes, questions or reading history. This policy explains what the app stores and what the services it uses can see.

What stays on your device or in your iCloud

  • Your data. Watched proposals, notes, questions, explanations, read state and Inbox progress are stored on your device. If iCloud is on, they sync through your private iCloud database, which only you can read.
  • Apple Intelligence. Explanations, answers and summaries are generated on your device. The text you ask about is not sent to the developer or to any third-party AI service.
  • Notifications. Notifications are scheduled on your device from the public Swift Evolution data. No push notification token is collected.

Public sources the app reads

To show proposals, the app downloads public content from swift.org, GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com and api.github.com) and the Swift Forums (forums.swift.org). Like any website, these services receive your IP address and standard request information under their own privacy policies.

Tips (Superwall)

The optional tip jar is shown by Superwall. When you open it, Superwall receives device and app information (such as device model, OS version, language, app version and an anonymous app-specific ID), events such as when the tip screen was shown or closed, and the outcome of a tip purchase. Apple processes the payment; neither the developer nor Superwall receives your payment details. This data is used to show the tip screen and to report tip totals, not to track you across other companies' apps.

Ads (Google AdMob)

On iPhone and iPad, SE Tracker may show a banner ad from Google AdMob in the Inbox and Library lists. To show and measure ads, Google may receive your IP address, device and app information, coarse location derived from your IP address, and how you interact with ads.

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, you are asked for consent first. You can change your choice any time in Settings › Ad Privacy Choices.
  • Your device's advertising identifier is shared only if you allow tracking when iOS asks. If you don't, ads are not personalised using that identifier.

The Mac app shows no ads.

Children

SE Tracker is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Your choices

  • Turn off iCloud for SE Tracker in the Settings app to keep your data on one device.
  • Deleting the app removes its data from the device. To delete synced data, remove SE Tracker's data in Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage.
  • Use Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking to control whether apps may ask to track you.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
