SE Tracker

Follow Swift Evolution proposals, reviews and releases on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Get help

Found a bug, have a question or want to suggest a feature? Open an issue on GitHub. Include your device, its OS version and the app version.

Common questions

Where does the data come from? SE Tracker reads the public Swift Evolution feed on swift.org, the proposal text on GitHub and the review threads on the Swift Forums.

Do my watches and notes sync? Yes, through your own iCloud account. Turn iCloud on for SE Tracker in the Settings app to sync between devices.

Why don't I see explanations? Explanations use Apple Intelligence on your device. They appear only on devices that support Apple Intelligence and have it turned on.

What do tips unlock? Nothing. Tips are optional one-time purchases that support development. SE Tracker stays free.
